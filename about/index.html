--- layout: default title: About description: Why DataRepublican exists, and who builds it. permalink: /about/ --- {% include section-header.html title="About" %} {%- comment -%} Two columns at lg: the narrative on the shared reading measure, the pull quotes in a rail across from it. Below lg the rail is simply the next thing down the page, which is why it carries its own top margin. The rail is the FIXED track and the narrative takes what is left, so the rail's right edge lands on the tweet wall's below it. Sizing the narrative instead — at `maxWidth.prose` — left the rail stopping ~170px short of the wall at 1400px, which read as a column that had failed to reach the edge. `.text-column` caps the paragraphs at the shared measure either way, so the narrative is no wider than it is on /donate/. `minmax(0,…)` on the flexible track keeps a long unbroken string from widening it, and `items-start` sits the rail at the top of the text rather than centering it against a column several times its height. {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} `.text-column` is the shared narrative measure — /donate/ uses the same class. Do not set a width here; change it in assets/css/main.css so both pages move. {%- endcomment -%}
