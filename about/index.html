--- layout: default title: About description: Why DataRepublican exists, and who builds it. permalink: /about/ --- {% include section-header.html title="About" %} {%- comment -%} Two columns at lg: the narrative on the shared reading measure, the pull quotes in a rail across from it. Below lg the rail is simply the next thing down the page, which is why it carries its own top margin. The narrative grows to the shared reading measure and STOPS; the rail is the `1fr` and takes everything past it. So at 1400px the text is exactly 52rem — the same measure `.text-column` caps it at, and the same one /donate/ uses — and every pixel after that belongs to the quotes. `minmax(19rem, 1fr)` rather than a bare `1fr` is what stops the rail collapsing at the bottom of this range: at 1024px there is not room for 52rem of text AND a rail, so the floor makes the text give way instead. `items-start` sits the rail at the top of the text rather than centering it against a column several times its height. Below lg the rail is simply the next thing down the page, and its own container query lays the two quotes out side by side once it has the width for them. {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} `.text-column` is the shared narrative measure — /donate/ uses the same class. Do not set a width here; change it in assets/css/main.css so both pages move. {%- endcomment -%}
