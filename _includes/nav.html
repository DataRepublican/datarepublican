{%- comment -%} The three-item pill. Tools is the home page, so there is no Home item. One DOM order, two layouts: the pill floats at the bottom of the viewport on a phone (thumb reach) and sticks near the top at md, just under the masthead, so it stays reachable down a long tool page. Sizing is anchored to the 16px label and expressed in em from there, so the pill is a consequence of the type rather than a set of numbers that drift away from it. At md that gives a 32px item in a 36px pill. The 44px minimum tap target is kept on phones, where the pill IS the navigation and the finger is the pointer. Above md there is a cursor and the compact pill applies — min-h-tap at every width made the bar half again as tall as the design called for. Hover fills the item the way the current item is already filled, so the two states read as the same control rather than two different treatments: an inactive item takes a flat tint, and the current item darkens its gradient instead of being the only item that does not respond. Both are colour-only transitions, so nothing shifts position under the cursor. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign here = page.url -%} {%- capture nav_items -%} /|Tools|tools,/about/|About|user,/donate/|Donate|donate {%- endcapture -%} {%- comment -%} The scrim keeps the pill legible over scrolling content. {%- endcomment -%}
