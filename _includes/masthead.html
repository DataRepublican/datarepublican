One element — the two bars are the top and bottom borders and the 3px height is the gap between them. This was `border-y-[3px] border-double`, which is not the same shape at all: CSS renders a 3px double border as 1px / 1px / 1px, so it drew four hairlines around a 5px gap instead of two bars of different weights. The height is the 10px TOTAL, not the 3px gap: Tailwind's preflight sets `box-sizing: border-box`, so the borders are drawn inside the height. Asking for h-[3px] gives 7px of border and no gap at all — one solid bar. {%- endcomment -%}
