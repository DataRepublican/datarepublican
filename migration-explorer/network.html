About this network & disclaimer

Limitations & disclaimer. Provided for research, educational, and journalistic purposes only. Compiled from public federal records (Census, ACS, CBP, USASpending, DHS, DOL, ORR, EOIR) and IRS filings, offered “as is” without warranty of any kind; it may contain errors or omissions. Foreign-born counts include naturalized citizens and every legal status – the Census does not ask legal status. Modeled estimates are labeled as such. Organizations appear because a public record documents federal money, a grant, or an affiliation; a listing describes that record only and alleges no wrongdoing. Dollar figures are documented minimums and dollar types are never added together. Every claim links to its source; please verify before relying on it.
