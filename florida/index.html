About this map

This map is for exploring who has voted early, by mail and in person, not an exact count. Counts by county, party and method come from the Florida Division of Elections turnout feed, which counties update about hourly. Broward is missing from that feed so far this year; until it appears, Broward's numbers come from the Broward Supervisor of Elections turnout dashboard.

Shift view colors each county by how far its D−R margin among the ticked ballot types has moved since the comparison point you pick. Same time (2024): the counts at the same hour on the same day before the election, from snapshots of the 2024 feed. Same day (2022): the end of the same day before the election (the 2022 feed has only daily totals; the party split comes from the Division's daily early voting and mail ballot reports). Final: every ballot of that type in that election. Margin view colors each county by its 2026 D−R margin alone; the baseline still sets the comparison numbers in the tiles and table.

Totals include every county. Counties with fewer than 10 ballots in 2026 or in the baseline are gray on the map.

"I" is every voter not registered D or R, including minor parties. The I split assigns each county's I ballots to R and D in that county's R:D ratio for the same ballots, then sums the counties. It is a heuristic, not a measurement.

Election results are the Division of Elections' certified county returns: 2024 President and 2022 Governor, the race on the 2026 ballot. Registration is the Division's monthly county report and its 2024 and 2022 general book-closing totals.
