About this map & disclaimer

A research map of the effective-altruism / AI-safety ecosystem, compiled from public sources. Nodes are organizations, projects, people, and concepts; edges are relationships drawn from public sources – each shown on the entity’s card with an evidence excerpt and a source link.

Lenses. Overview colors nodes by community. Money flow shows publicly reported funding (capital → grantmakers → recipients). EA affiliation places entities by how strong their documented link to EA is – strongest at the center.

How categories are assigned. A person’s EA affiliation is graded from public evidence and shown with the receipt: self-identification in their own words, a documented role at an EA organization, participation in EA forums or events, or receipt of EA-affiliated funding. Organizations and projects are graded the same way. A large independent institution – a university, a government body, or a major commercial lab – is not labeled EA merely because EA-affiliated people work there or it has received EA money; that is EA reaching into an institution, not the institution being EA. Ideology tags (longtermist, rationalist, AI-safety) are tracked separately and are not folded into EA affiliation.
